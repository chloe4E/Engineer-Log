## Today I Learned

Gemini suggested 4 weeks plan to onboard senior topics:

### Week 1 — System Design

| Day | Focus | Activity |
|-----|-------|----------|
| 1 | Foundations | Review CAP Theorem, Load Balancing strategies, and Caching layers (Write-through vs. Back). |
| 2 | Microservices | Study Service Discovery, API Gateways, and Resilience patterns (Circuit Breakers). |
| 3 | Scalability | Practice a "Design WhatsApp" or "Design Uber" prompt. Focus on Sharding and Consistent Hashing. |
| 4 | Trade-offs | Read "The Log" or a technical blog from Netflix/Uber on a specific architectural failure. |

### Week 2 — Databases & Messaging

| Day | Focus | Activity |
|-----|-------|----------|
| 1 | Relational | Indexes (B-Trees), Isolation Levels, and Transactions. Understand why a GIN index differs from B-Tree. |
| 2 | NoSQL | Deep dive into LSM Trees (Cassandra/LevelDB) vs. Document stores. Know when to go Schema-less. |
| 3 | Messaging | Event-driven architecture. Kafka (Pub/Sub, Partitions) vs. RabbitMQ (Smart Broker). |
| 4 | Consistency | Strong vs. Eventual consistency. Study Distributed Locks and Consensus (Raft/Paxos) basics. |

### Week 3 — Algorithms & Concurrency

| Day | Focus | Activity |
|-----|-------|----------|
| 1 | Sliding Window | Solve 2 "Medium" problems. Focus on substring/subarray optimization. |
| 2 | Graphs & BFS/DFS | Solve 2 problems related to topological sorting or shortest paths (Dijkstra). |
| 3 | Concurrency | Language-specific: Study Thread safety, Mutexes, Semaphores, and Deadlocks in your primary language. |
| 4 | System API Design | Practice designing a REST or gRPC API for a simple service. Focus on Idempotency and Pagination. |

### Week 4 — Behavioural & Polish

| Day | Focus | Activity |
|-----|-------|----------|
| 1 | STAR Method | Prepare 3 stories: A technical conflict, a major failure/rollback, and a mentorship win. |
| 2 | Project Deep-Dive | Pick your "shining star" project. Map out the architecture, the biggest bottleneck, and what you’d change today. |
| 3 | Mock Interview | Record yourself explaining a system design. Watch for "ums" and clarify your decision-making logic. |
| 4 | The Final Polish | Review your resume. Ensure every bullet point has a metric (e.g., "Reduced latency by 20%"). |
